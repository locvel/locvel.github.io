Our Values

🔒

Privacy First

Your data never leaves your device. No accounts, no servers, no tracking. What you create stays yours.

📱

Local First

Full functionality offline. Your apps work without internet, without permissions, without compromises.

🌐

Open Source

Transparent, auditable, community-driven. Every line of code is public and free to inspect.

End-to-end encryption, secure storage, minimal permissions. Security is built in, not bolted on.

Sustainable

Free forever. No subscriptions, no ads, no data mining. Just tools that respect you.
